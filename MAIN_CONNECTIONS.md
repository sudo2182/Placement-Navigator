# 🎯 Main Database Connections - Quick Overview

## **The 3 Main Connection Hubs:**

### **1. USERS (Central Hub)**
**Connects to everything:**
- **Jobs** - TPO posts jobs
- **Applications** - Students apply for jobs  
- **AI Matches** - Students get matched to jobs
- **Notifications** - Students receive updates
- **Courses** - Faculty create, students register
- **Resources** - Faculty upload materials

### **2. JOBS (Recruitment Hub)**
**Connects to recruitment process:**
- **Applications** - Students apply for jobs
- **AI Matches** - AI finds compatible students
- **Events** - Tests, interviews scheduled
- **Shortlists** - Students selected for rounds
- **Notifications** - Job-related updates

### **3. APPLICATIONS (Action Hub)**
**Connects students to jobs:**
- **Users** - Which student applied
- **Jobs** - Which job they applied for
- **AI Matches** - AI compatibility scores
- **Shortlists** - Selection for interviews

## **Key Flow:**
**User → Application → Job → Events → Shortlists → Placement**

## **Supporting Connections:**
- **Courses:** Faculty create → Students register
- **Resources:** Faculty upload → Students access
- **Notifications:** System sends → Students receive
- **Opt-out:** Students submit → TPO reviews

## **The Big Picture:**
1. **Users** are the center of everything
2. **Jobs** drive the recruitment process
3. **Applications** connect students to opportunities
4. **Everything else** supports this main flow

**Simple: Users apply for Jobs, get matched by AI, go through Events, get Shortlisted, and placed!** 🎯
